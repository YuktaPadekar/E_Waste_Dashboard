Data quality note

!

The sheet's STATE TOTAL row is overstated. It sums the six real districts plus the greyed‑out District A template/example row (population 2,500,000; 380,000 kg). That inflates the printed total from 310,500 kg (six actual districts) to 690,500 kg, and population from 855,000 to 3,355,000.

Every figure and chart on this dashboard uses the corrected, districts‑only total of 310,500 kg across a covered population of 855,000. Recommend deleting or clearly excluding the template row at source before the next reporting cycle.

Reading this chart

 

Mangan generates the least waste in absolute terms (28,000 kg) but has the highest per‑capita rate in the state (560 g/person) — over 50% above the state average — despite the smallest population. Gangtok is the largest absolute generator (38.6% of the state total, roughly matching its 37.7% share of population), so its per‑capita rate sits close to average. Namchi is the lowest per‑capita district at 313 g/person.
